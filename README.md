# 🏛️ Apex Global University — Campus Web Portal

A modern, responsive, and interactive collegiate web portal showcasing academic programs, student planning tools (GPA Calculator & Tuition Estimator), campus life, admissions timeline, and an interactive virtual campus guide assistant.

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **🌓 Dual Theme Experience**: Native support for **Light** (regal collegiate navy & ivory) and **Dark** (nocturnal obsidian & luminous cobalt) themes with persistent preference storage.
- **🎓 Academic Programs Explorer**: Filter programs across Computer Science & AI, Robotics, Quantitative Finance, Biomedical Genomics, and Sustainable Architecture.
- **📊 Interactive Semester & Cumulative GPA Calculator**:
  - Add/delete courses, adjust credits, select letter grades (A+ to F).
  - Automatically computes quality points, total credits, term GPA, and Latin Honors standing (*Summa Cum Laude*, *Magna Cum Laude*, *Cum Laude*).
- **💰 Tuition & Merit Scholarship Estimator**:
  - Calculate estimated tuition, room & board, and automatic merit scholarship deductions based on residency, program level, and GPA slider.
- **🤖 Virtual Campus AI Assistant (Chatbot)**:
  - Interactive floating widget providing instant answers on scholarships, housing, admissions deadlines, and GPA tools.
- **🏛️ Admissions & Inquiry Portals**:
  - Interactive modal application submission and prospective student inquiry forms with toast confirmations.
- **📅 Upcoming Campus Events Calendar**:
  - Hackathons, keynote lectures, and research symposium cards with RSVP triggers.
- **⚡ 100% Client-Side & Zero Dependencies**:
  - Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, perfectly optimized for static hosting on **GitHub Pages**.
